    OH MY FRIEND GANESHA

    Celebrate Ganesh Chaturthi with a purpose by welcoming an eco-friendly Ganesha idol that inspires positive change beyond the festival. Every idol you purchase helps provide clean drinking water projects in underserved communities, empowers underprivileged women through sewing and skill-development training, and supports the education of disadvantaged children by improving access to quality learning resources and opportunities. Together, your devotion becomes a force for compassion, sustainability, and brighter futures—making every celebration a step towards building stronger communities and transforming lives.

    About the Initiative

    Oh My Friend Ganesha is the flagship fundraising initiative of the Rotaract Club of Hyderabad East, transforming every Ganesh Chaturthi celebration into an opportunity to create meaningful social impact. Every eco-friendly Ganesha idol purchased helps support clean drinking water projects, women empowerment through skill development and sustainable livelihood opportunities, and quality education for underprivileged children. By bringing Lord Ganesha home, you are not just celebrating a festival—you are contributing to a future where communities thrive, lives are transformed, and hope reaches those who need it most.
