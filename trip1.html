Explore

Things to Do

Explore some of the experiences and places planned as part of the trip.

🏖️

Gokarna Beach

Spend time by the coast and experience the relaxed atmosphere of Gokarna.

🌴

Om Beach

Enjoy one of Gokarna's well-known coastal destinations and its beautiful surroundings.

🌅

Kudle Beach

Relax, walk along the shore, and enjoy the coastal sunset.

🛕

Murudeshwar Temple

Visit the iconic temple and explore the surrounding coastal landscape.

🏔️

Yana Caves

Explore the unique rock formations and natural surroundings of Yana.

🌿

Nature & Local Experiences

Discover local food, markets, landscapes, and the quieter side of the region.

Before You Go

Trip Notes

🎒 What to Carry

  • Comfortable clothes and footwear
  • Personal medicines and essentials
  • Water bottle
  • Sunscreen and basic toiletries
  • Power bank and charging accessories
  • Valid ID proof

ℹ️ Important Information

  • Final timings may change based on travel and local conditions.
  • Accommodation will be assigned based on availability.
  • Please follow instructions given by the trip coordinators.
  • Participants are responsible for carrying their personal belongings.
  • The final itinerary will be shared before departure.
