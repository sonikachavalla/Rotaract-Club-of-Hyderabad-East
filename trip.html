About the Getaway

Gokarna is known for its beautiful coastline, relaxed beaches, scenic landscapes, and cultural heritage. This trip brings together some of the most interesting experiences around Gokarna and the surrounding region.

The journey is designed not only around sightseeing, but also around spending time together, exploring new places, and creating memories as a group.

From beaches and waterfalls to temples, backwaters, and coastal landscapes, the trip offers a balance of adventure, relaxation, and exploration.

Explore

Things to Do

Explore beaches, nature, temples, waterfalls, and scenic coastal destinations during the trip.

01

Gokarna Beach

Spend time along one of Gokarna's popular coastal stretches and enjoy the relaxed beach atmosphere.

02

Om Beach

Explore the iconic beach known for its distinctive shape, coastal views, and peaceful surroundings.

03

Kudle Beach

Enjoy the scenic coastline and relaxed atmosphere of this popular beach near Gokarna.

04

Murudeshwar Temple

Visit the famous temple complex and experience its impressive coastal setting.

05

Yana Caves

Discover the striking rock formations and natural surroundings of the Yana region.

06

Honnavara Backwaters

Experience the calm coastal backwaters and surrounding landscapes around Honnavara.

07

Vibhooti Waterfalls

Spend time in nature and explore the scenic waterfall surroundings.

08

Eco Beach

Explore another beautiful stretch of the Karnataka coastline and enjoy the natural surroundings.

09

Coastal Exploration

Take in the landscapes, local surroundings, and memorable views throughout the journey.

Journey

Itinerary

A planned journey covering the major experiences across the trip.

Day 01

Journey to Gokarna

Begin the journey towards Gokarna. Settle in, explore the surroundings, and start experiencing the coastal atmosphere of the destination.

Day 02

Beaches & Gokarna Exploration

Explore the popular beaches around Gokarna, including Gokarna Beach, Om Beach, and Kudle Beach, while enjoying the coastal scenery.

Day 03

Nature & Coastal Experiences

Explore destinations around the region, including natural landscapes, waterfalls, backwaters, and other scenic locations depending on the final trip schedule.

Day 04

Murudeshwar & Return Journey

Visit Murudeshwar and its famous temple before beginning the return journey, bringing the Gokarna getaway to an end.
