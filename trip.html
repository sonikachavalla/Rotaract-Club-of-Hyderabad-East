Explore

Things to Do

Explore some of the experiences and places planned as part of the trip.

🏖️

Gokarna Beach

Relax by the Arabian Sea and experience Gokarna's peaceful coastal atmosphere.

🌴

Om Beach

Admire the iconic Om-shaped coastline, enjoy the beach, and take in the scenic views.

🌅

Kudle Beach

Take a peaceful walk along the wide sandy shore and enjoy a beautiful sunset by the sea.

🛕

Murudeshwar Temple

Visit the famous Shiva temple, see the towering Shiva statue, and enjoy views of the Arabian Sea.

🏔️

Yana Caves

Explore the striking black limestone rock formations surrounded by lush forests and scenic hiking trails.

🏖️

Paradise Beach

Escape to this scenic beach with golden sands, rocky surroundings, and a peaceful coastal setting.

🏔️

Half Moon Beach

Enjoy a quieter coastal escape with a small sandy cove, surrounded by rocks and lush greenery.

🌅

Belekan Beach

Discover a secluded beach near Gokarna, known for its peaceful atmosphere, natural beauty, and sunsets.

🌿

Nature & Local Experiences

Experience local food, village life, scenic landscapes, temples, markets, and the relaxed culture of the region.
