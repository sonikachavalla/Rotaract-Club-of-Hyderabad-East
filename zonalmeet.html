The Gathering

Event Overview

A day to come together, connect and strengthen fellowship across Kakatiya Zone.

The Kakatiya Zonal Meet is an opportunity for Rotaractors from clubs across District 3150 to meet, interact and celebrate the spirit of fellowship. Hosted by Rotaract Club of Hyderabad East, the gathering brings the zone together under one roof.
