About RODA

RODA - Rotaract District Awards is the annual flagship celebration of Rotaract District 3150, bringing together Rotaractors from across the district to recognize achievements, celebrate impact, and honor excellence across various avenues. More than just an awards event, RODA is a platform for fellowship, networking, inspiration, and celebrating the incredible journey of clubs and individuals throughout the Rotary year.
